1. Preorder traversal: 50, 30, 20, 40, 70, 60, 65, 80
2. Inorder traversal: 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 65, 70, 80
3. Postorder traversal: 20, 40, 30, 65, 60, 80, 70, 50

4. Inorder traversal gives the numbers in sorted order because, 
in a binary search tree, smaller values are placed in the left subtree 
and larger values are placed in the right subtree.
